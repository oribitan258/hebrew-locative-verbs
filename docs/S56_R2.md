## 2. How each part works

**Units (D-059 P1–P5).**
- **Automatic link.** A lemma joins another only when it is that verb's present participle in the same binyan, has
  no other reading, and Dicta uses it mainly for the present tense. Example: רואה → ראה.
- **Reviewed link.** Everything else waits for a human: passives (נמסר → מסר), reflexives (התפזר → פיזר), strings
  with two readings (רוצה: qal "wants" or pu'al), and inflected strings.
- **Before the audit.** No automatic link is in force until the audit has passed. Development runs can force them
  (`--provisional-units`), and the manifest records it.
- **Paper verbs** are never in the map: the lexicon decides them.

**Extractor (§5.3).**
- `verb_key` keeps its I3 meaning (lexicon key, `lemma:<lemma>`, `form:<host>`); `unit_id` carries the D-059 unit.
- The DO, PP and bare-NP slots are read by R1's unchanged `tree.arguments_sql`. Passive subjects count as the
  object when the voice is passive or middle.
- Flags (never filters): `partitive_candidate`, `instrument_candidate`, `al_for_b`, `conj_shared`, `tree_invalid`,
  `multi_piece`, `function_lemma`.
- `function_use` marks a function-predicate token with structural evidence: a clausal or infinitival complement
  and no object, or an existential/copular use with no object.
- For third-person pronouns, the nearest preceding gender/number-matching noun is recorded (for R2).

**Resolver (§5.4, rule zero as in D-059 P7).**
- **What it reads.** A fixed list of columns that contains no preposition. Its only preposition-derived input is the
  boolean `lone_min_complement`.
- **The fixed order.** Structural rules (idiom, clausal complement, DO + indefinite bare NP, partitive with the
  LOCATION guard) → noun types → complementary inference between the DO and one PP (two or more typed PPs give
  AMBIGUOUS) → DO only.
- **Every outcome** carries a `reason` and a `decided_by` list.

**Classes and identity (§5.5–§5.6).**
- **Classes.** The fixed precedence is computed at k = 1, 2, 3, at the unit and the lemma level, and with and without
  function-use tokens.
- **Identity.** It reads only the Location PP of a full Frame A instance and the Locatum PP of a full Frame B
  instance. Partitive and bare-NP instances have no PP in that role, so they never decide identity.
  - `OTHER` is compared by its exact preposition.
  - The test is also reported with על counted as ב (fn 22).
- **Swap test.** Role-free: nouns that are both the DO and the object of a family's PP for the same verb.

**Test independence (D-059 P8).**
- Validation is read only by the once-only variant choice, and test only by the once-only `--final-test`. Both are
  scored on the clean sets.
- Paper examples that are frozen validation or test tokens are left out of the dev set.

## 4. Known limitations (Phase B)

1. **No noun types yet** (`config/noun_types.yaml` does not exist). The resolver therefore decides mostly by its
   structural rules, and most instances are UNDETERMINED. **No class or identity number is a result** until the
   noun types are merged and Phase C has run.
2. **R1's identification limits are unchanged** (S56_R1.md §0.1). Phase C candidates I-1–I-5 address them on dev.
3. **The unit links are not in force** (audit pending; reviews not done). §6 numbers would be per raw lemma for most
   verbs until then.
4. **Two typed PPs give AMBIGUOUS** (P7 literal). How often this happens is reported; any refinement needs Ori's
   decision.
5. **Voice by shape** is conservative (only hof'al). Passive subjects of non-lexicon nif'al/pu'al verbs are not read
   until a reviewed link gives their voice.
6. **The R2 antecedent search** covers the same unit and the previous unit of the same run only.
7. **Modern at full scale** has not been run in Phase B (timing from one or two slices only).

**The safeguards.**
- **SG1:** isolated workspaces outside the repo, a restricted headless launch, deny rules, a canary preflight, and a
  transcript access log.
- **SG2:** a freeze, hashes recorded before any read, and a scorer that refuses anything else.
- **SG3:** the pre-registered recovery, with a STOP for project-level decisions only.

All three are in `annotation/S56_SILVER_PROTOCOL.md` and `hebloc.loc.silver`.

**Scoring.** `loc score` reads SILVER by default (`--label-source silver`).
- Every figure is "agreement with SILVER labels — not validated accuracy".
- Abstained items are left out of the primary frame metrics and counted.
- An `agreed_only` sensitivity set is reported.
- Validation (`--choose`) and test (`--final-test`) are still read once each, on the clean sets.
